Submission checklist

  • Use headings matching the assignment; cite sources beside the claims they support.
  • Include student/team names; upload a readable .docx or .pdf for written reflections and a PDF for project reports.
  • For videos, use the existing upload/link field and confirm playback permissions.
  • Open the uploaded file and confirm the assignment shows submitted.
  • Save your decision record and contribution notes; use the static packet if SkillSimm is unavailable.

Venture workshops and feedback

Thursday’s last 20 minutes produce a small piece of the existing team project, not another separately graded assignment. Teams that finish simulations early can begin the workshop. Feedback names a criterion, a specific strength, and a concrete next revision.
