Team Project — Evaluate a Startup Plan and Improve It

Start here

Keep one five-person team and one supplied startup plan across the midterm and final. Choose Roarmic, Marketing Email Agent, or Alien Chef Arena. Treat the supplied plan’s claims as assumptions to test.

  • Midterm: full instructions, checklist, report rubric, and pitch rubric
  • Final: full instructions, checklist, report rubric, and pitch rubric
  • Course assignment guide and grading weights

Each project contributes 20% of the course: report 10%, video 10%. Each report is scored out of 100 with up to 5 report bonus points; each video is scored out of 100. The linked documents are the authoritative criteria rather than a second rubric here.

Work and submit

Use one shared Google Doc: Part 1 for the midterm, Part 2 for the final. Four members draft their assigned sections; the Team Leader integrates the report. Each member saves and submits an accurate contribution summary in SkillSimm. The Team Leader coordinates the report PDF and 15-minute team video submission in Blackboard. The final’s report sections are A–E, with A owned by the leader.

Preserve the existing deadlines and video milestones in HuskyCT. If SkillSimm is unavailable, retain your own PDF and contribution notes and use the existing Blackboard submissions. Save a PDF before closing a project run.

Read and revise

Start with assumptions, risks, financial details, and operations in the supplied plan before evaluating its executive summary. Test claims against outside evidence and cite the supplied plan by section. Use the research guidance in the linked assignment documents.

Keep instructor comment → revision → location notes. Each Thursday workshop adds a small, useful component to this same report.
