Account Manager — Company Overview

Detailed reference, not required in full during the timed activity. Start with the short student packet; use this page to investigate or cite supporting evidence.

Case: Marvell Technology, Inc., Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026

You answer no functional question of your own. The other four leads each own one decision and one page. You own the whole company — and the order in which its problems get solved. Your page is the overview. Read it before they present.

The company, in the filing’s own terms

“We are a leading supplier of data infrastructure semiconductor solutions, spanning the data center core to network edge. We are a fabless supplier of high-performance semiconductor products with core strengths in developing and scaling complex System-on-a-Chip architectures.”

— Item 7, Management’s Discussion and Analysis

It designs the silicon. Somebody else manufactures it — “most of our current qualified integrated circuit foundries are located in the same region within Taiwan”, with assembly, test and packaging in the Pacific Rim. It sells to OEMs, original design manufacturers and distributors, and operates in one reportable segment.

How to run the room

  • Rank by reversibility, not by volume of voice. Ask of every proposal: how long would it take to stop, and what would stopping cost?
  • You may not rank #1 anything a lead could not defend with a number.
  • Watch for the margin story. Somebody will argue from a 32.6% net margin. The operating margin was 16.1%; the difference is a $1.8 billion gain on selling a business, and it happens once.
  • Watch for the growth story. Somebody will want to reserve more capacity because data center grew 46%. Ask what fiscal 2027 looks like if the forecast is twenty percent high — and remind them that $1,871.8 million of $2,665.8 million already falls in that one year.
  • Watch for the two speeds. Customers cancel “with relatively short notice.” Foundries are booked for 4 to 10 years. Revenue falls fast; costs fall slowly. That gap is the crisis.
  • Watch for the leftover-budget story. Somebody will fund security last. Ask what a $1.87 billion capacity commitment is worth for products we may not be permitted to ship.
  • You are allowed to disagree with your own team. The ranking is yours to sign.
