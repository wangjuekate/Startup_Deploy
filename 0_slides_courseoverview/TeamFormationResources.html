Simulation: Finance of a two-person software startup

How this works

  • Take the role you were assigned and join that group.
  • Everyone works from the same one-page brief. Each group answers only its own role's question.
  • Read silently first and pick one option. No discussion yet.
  • Agree one answer as a group, then select a representative to present it.
  • Representatives come to the front and sit for a panel interview.

Goal of the simulation

At today's numbers, how far is Cadence from paying for itself, and what would it take to close the gap?

Cadence is a two-person software startup. Two founders, no outside money, one product, and a bank balance that is going in the wrong direction.

The Startup

Users and pricing

1,000 free users.

80 paid users.

1,080 total active users.

$20 per month per paid user.

Costs

$6,000 per month in fixed cost.

$2 per month in variable cost for every active user.

Free and paid users both create variable cost.

These are all the numbers there are. Everything your role is asked comes out of this slide.

The panel

  • Join with classmates who have the same role.
  • As a group of the same role, discuss about your decisions and reasons.
  • Select one representative to come and sit on the front for a interview about what we have learned.
💰

Financial Lead

What is monthly revenue?

  • A $1,600
  • B $6,000
  • C $8,000
  • D $20,000
💰

Financial Lead — Key Takeaways

  • Revenue counts only the users who actually pay. Of 1,080 active users, 80 carry the entire top line.
  • A big user base and a big revenue number are two different claims. Be clear about which one you are quoting.
  • Price times paying customers is the whole formula. Anything added to it is a forecast, not revenue.
📣

Operation Lead

What is total variable cost if all 1,080 active users create cost?

  • A $160
  • B $2,160
  • C $6,000
  • D $8,160
📣

Operation Lead — Key Takeaways

  • What is left of a paying user after variable cost is the only money available to cover fixed cost.
📣

Market Lead

As the Market Lead, select the main business issue given the unit economics

  • A Too many users
  • B Not enough paid conversion
  • C Price too high
  • D No fixed cost
📣

Market Lead — Key Takeaways

  • Cost follows usage, not billing. Every active user consumes the product whether or not they pay for it.
  • Free users are an expense line with a growth story attached. Both halves of that sentence are real.
🧭

Account Manager

Rank these four levers 1–4, best to worst, for reaching break-even. Use each lever once, no repeats.

  • A Increase paid conversion
  • B Reduce fixed cost
  • C Increase price
  • D Add more free users
🧭

Account Manager — Key Takeaways

  • A ranking is a claim about which constraint binds hardest, not a list of good ideas.
  • Moving conversion changes the slope of the business. Trimming fixed cost shifts the line down once.
  • Growth that adds cost without adding revenue moves you away from break-even. Scale is not a strategy by itself.
