MENT 3235 · Module 1 — Marketing Research and the Value Chain Case: TheRealReal, Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025

You own one decision: which revenue line the next dollar of growth spending feeds. Everyone on your team has a different page. Yours is the money. Read it, pick one option, and be ready to defend it with a number.

Read the bottom two rows together. The operating loss is $23.9 million. The net loss is $41.8 million because of $27.7 million of interest expense. After fourteen years, this company is one good decision away from making money.

The number that should stop you

There are three revenue lines, and they are not the same business.

“When we accept out of policy returns from buyers, or when we make direct purchases from businesses and consignors, we take ownership of goods and retain 100% of the proceeds when the goods subsequently sell.”

— Item 7, Management’s Discussion and Analysis

That sentence is true. Read it next to the table anyway.

And the fastest-growing line is…

Direct revenue went from $64.6 million to $91.1 million — up 41%, the fastest growth of any line in the company. Consignment grew 13%.

What the company charges

“In 2025 and 2024, our overall take rate on consigned goods was 37.7% and 38.4% respectively. The decrease in our take rate was due to sales mix into higher value items.”

— Item 7

Consignors kept the rest — “an overall commission rate of approximately 62% in 2025.”

One more thing worth noticing

$2.13 billion of goods moved across the marketplace. $692.8 million of it became revenue — about 32.5 cents of every GMV dollar. GMV is not revenue, and the filing says so directly: “While GMV is the primary driver of our revenue, it is not a proxy for revenue or revenue growth.”

Your question

The company is $23.9 million from operating breakeven. Where should the next dollar of growth spending go?

Your four choices — pick one

  • (A) Nowhere yet. We are $23.9 million from breakeven and paying $27.7 million of interest — retire debt first, then invest.
  • (B) Behind consignment supply — it returns about 89 cents on the revenue dollar against roughly 22 for direct, so the same growth is worth four times as much there.
  • (C) Into the authentication centres, so we can push more consigned volume through the same 40%-of-revenue cost base — that is where the operating leverage is.
  • (D) Behind direct — it grew 41% and we keep 100% of the proceeds instead of 37.7%.

Before you answer

A competent lead does four things, in this order:

  1. Name the step on the chain your choice touches. SOURCE → VERIFY → PRICE → SELL → SHIP → RETURNS.
  2. Cite at least one figure from this page. Not a feeling — a number.
  3. Name what your choice gives up. Every option costs you something.
  4. State one thing that would change your answer. If nothing would, you are not reasoning.

Your teammates will argue that direct revenue is growing fastest and that keeping 100% beats keeping 37.7%. Both statements are true. Ask yourself what the company paid for the goods before it kept the 100%.
