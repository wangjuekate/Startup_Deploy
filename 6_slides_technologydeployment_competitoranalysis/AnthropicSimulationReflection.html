Cerebras Against the Moat

Simulation Reflection · Thursday, October 29

Group Discussion

  • Form groups with classmates who have the same role.
  • As a group, say whether your choice grows our advantage or shrinks their switching cost — and name the dimension the buyer is bleeding on.
  • Select one representative to present your group's choice and defense.
💰

Financial Lead

Revenue +76% to $510.0 M. Gross margin fell 42.3% → 39.0%. Loss from operations widened to $(145.9) M. Reported net income $237.8 M came from $390.7 M of other income; non-GAAP is a $75.7 M loss. R&D is 48% of revenue. What do you do with the money?

  • A Fix the margin on the growing line — cloud grew 94% at ~29.9% against hardware's ~42.9%.
  • B Raise and spend on data-centre capacity; we cannot deliver the OpenAI tranches without it.
  • C Protect R&D at 48% of revenue whatever else happens.
  • D Fund growth out of the $237.8 M of net income and avoid dilution.
💰

Financial Lead — Key Takeaways

  • The profit is not from selling computers: $237.8 M of net income sits on a $(145.9) M operating loss and $390.7 M of other income. "Now that we're profitable" is built on an accounting entry.
  • Growth arrived in the line that keeps least — cloud +94% at ~29.9% against hardware's ~42.9% — which is exactly why total margin fell in a year revenue rose 76%.
  • There is no operating profit to fund anything with.
📣

Marketing Lead

MBZUAI 62.0% + G42 24.0% = 86% of 2025 revenue, from two related parties; one is 77.9% of receivables. OpenAI's MRA is future revenue. AWS will deploy Cerebras in its own data centres, and inference already ships through six marketplaces and gateways. Top ten customers grew spend ~80% in 12 months. Where does go-to-market effort go?

  • A Into the channels buyers already use — AWS, the marketplaces and gateways.
  • B Into landing more flagship names like the OpenAI agreement.
  • C Into the benchmark story — publish 15× and 10× results everywhere.
  • D Into deepening the customers we already have; they grew 80% in twelve months.
📣

Marketing Lead — Key Takeaways

  • Being purchasable inside AWS and the standard gateways shrinks the customer's cost of trying — the side of the inequality the company can move fastest — and it produces many small buyers, the only real answer to 86% in two related parties.
  • Deepening existing customers is exactly how the company arrived at 86% of revenue in two related parties and 77.9% of receivables in one — curing concentration by concentrating.
  • More proof of the thing already proven (15×) does not move the decision.
⚙️

Operation Lead

The MRA requires "capacity tranches… upon certain time-based milestones"; failure lets OpenAI "terminate a portion or all of the agreement" and accelerates a $1.0 billion loan. Chips come from TSMC under a fabless model; sole-source components are bought "on a purchase order basis without capacity or volume commitments"; data centres are leased and not controlled. Where does operational effort go?

  • A Convert the supply chain from purchase orders to committed capacity.
  • B Into the data-centre build — leased space is the step between contract and delivery.
  • C Into yield and throughput at Sunnyvale, the part of the chain we own.
  • D Keep the flexible model; purchase orders let a company our size scale with demand.
⚙️

Operation Lead — Key Takeaways

  • The company has promised dated tranches on inputs bought without commitments — failure lets OpenAI terminate and accelerates the $1.0 billion loan: the largest single downside anywhere in the case.
  • Flexibility with no volume commitments is right for a company with no delivery obligations — this company has dated obligations with a termination right and a debt trigger attached.
  • Leased racks are useless without the wafers to fill them.
💻

Tech Lead

58× larger, 2,625× bandwidth, 15× inference, 10× training. The compiler already eliminates the need for CUDA and runs PyTorch without rewriting code. R&D is $243.3 M — 48% of revenue. Where does the engineering go?

  • A Into making the switch invisible — unchanged PyTorch, standard APIs, existing toolchains.
  • B Into the cloud software platform — orchestration, multi-tenancy, utilisation.
  • C Into the next generation of the WSE.
  • D Into benchmarks and reference designs that make the advantage unarguable.
💻

Tech Lead — Key Takeaways

  • The advantage side is already enormous; the unsolved side is everything a customer would abandon. Adoption engineering converts a benchmark into an order.
  • Going from 15× to 20× buys nothing from a buyer who was not blocked on speed.
  • Proving harder what is already proven spends engineering on the side of the inequality that is already winning.
🔒

Security Lead

Exports need licences the company "cannot guarantee" obtaining; BIS may change the rules "at any time"; most 2025 revenue came from two UAE-linked related parties. The company disclosed material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting, and its leased data centres face "break-ins, human error, malfeasance or interference." Where do you set the bar?

  • A Export-control and trade compliance as a first-class function, plus remediating the internal-control weaknesses.
  • B The leased data centres and the customer workloads running on them.
  • C Protecting the wafer-scale designs and process know-how.
  • D Keep it proportionate — 708 people with a queue of demand; scale compliance later.
🔒

Security Lead — Key Takeaways

  • A licence problem does not slow the company down — it removes, by law, the geography holding 86% of last year's revenue.
  • "We're only 708 people" is exactly the argument that produces material weaknesses — which this company has already disclosed.
  • Losing the IP is a multi-year erosion; losing a licence is next quarter.

Account Manager

You do not answer a functional question.

After all five leads present their selection + one-sentence defense, rank the five decisions 1–5 by which the company must resolve first, allocate 100 points across them in blocks of 10, and name the dimension: what is the buyer actually bleeding on, and is our advantage on that dimension bigger than everything the buyer would have to throw away?

🧭

Account Manager — Key Takeaways

  • Rank by what would stop the company selling — delivery and concentration belong at the top, and the rank-#1 sentence must contain a figure.
  • The buyer is bleeding on switching cost, not on speed — 15× has already cleared the threshold.
  • The four multiples are not the same claim: 58× (chip size), 2,625× (bandwidth), 15× (inference), 10× (training) — only the last two are things a customer experiences.

Key takeaway

A genuine technical advantage must beat the incumbent's ecosystem — the complements, the habits, the switching cost — not just its silicon. Grow the side of the inequality that is small; the side that is already winning needs no more effort.

Fifteen times faster is a real advantage. The question is whether it is a business.
